# Sperner's Lemma and Fixed Points of Multivalued Maps on Cubes

Yitzchak Shmalo

Let $f$ be an upper semicontinuous map of the cube $[-1,1]^d$ with nonempty compact convex values. Choose $y_v\in f(v)$ at the
vertices of a cubical subdivision and label each vertex by the coordinate signs of $y_v-v$. Some cube then carries all $2^d$
labels or has a facet whose labels are not the corners on one side of a hyperplane, and along subdivisions of vanishing mesh
some sequence of such cubes, or of such facets, shrinks to a fixed point of $f$. No Lipschitz bound is assumed. The existence
of such a cube follows from a Sperner lemma for cells labeled by the vertices of a polytope, under a nonzero boundary degree
and a condition on the labels of each facet.
